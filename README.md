# Sports Club Management System (Karate & Gymnastics)

This project is a comprehensive Sports Club Management System specifically designed for the club manager to streamline daily operations in athletic facilities specializing exclusively in two core disciplines: Karate and Gymnastics. The primary objective of this application is to replace traditional manual paperwork with an organized, digital, and efficient workflow that gives the club manager complete visibility and control over all administrative tasks.
To achieve smooth operational management, the system integrates five essential modules into a single unified platform. At the core of the application is the players management module, which maintains organized digital profiles for all club members, allowing the manager to easily record, update, and track their essential personal information and contact details within either the Karate or Gymnastics department. Alongside this, the coaches management module organizes instructor records by assigning each coach to their respective sport department, tracking their specializations, and managing their assigned training groups. Furthermore, the subscriptions and billing module handles membership plans, monthly or seasonal renewals, payment statuses, and automated tracking of active and expired subscriptions to ensure accurate financial oversight.
In addition to member and financial management, the system provides a schedules and timetables module that allows the club manager to create, update, and coordinate daily and weekly training sessions for both Karate dojos and Gymnastics halls without any time conflicts. Finally, the equipment and inventory module monitors the availability, condition, and maintenance needs of essential training gear, such as gymnastics mats, safety pads, karate punching bags, and protective equipment. Overall, this system enhances the club manager's productivity, minimizes scheduling and administrative errors, and provides a reliable digital foundation for running a specialized Karate and Gymnastics club.
